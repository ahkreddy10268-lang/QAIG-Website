## Not sourced from official channels (all 40)
No logo was retrieved from an official website or brand-resource page. Every asset is a raster crop of the supplied board, so:
- Resolution is limited to the board (logos are 47–170 px tall before 2× upscaling). Small text inside seals (DoT, IIT Hyderabad, AIMSCS, government emblems) is soft.
- Monochrome conversion was automatic. Check DRDO, INCHFAB, T-Works and AMTZ, where solid colour fills become solid white shapes. AMTZ keeps a thin rule from its yellow panel.
- Some organizations (government emblems, IIT seals, JPMorgan Chase, Morgan Stanley, ABB) have brand rules on recolouring. Confirm permission or swap to the official monochrome file.
